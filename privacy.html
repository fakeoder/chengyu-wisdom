Privacy policy

A clear account of what happens when you spend time with our collection of stories.

Our approach

Chengyu Wisdom is a reading-focused cultural reference site. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell personal information.

Information we collect

We may receive basic, non-identifying technical information from our hosting provider, such as browser type, approximate location, and pages requested. This information is used to keep the site secure and reliable.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your theme preference in your browser's local storage. This preference stays on your device and is not sent to us. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking profiles.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@zkraft.cc. This policy was last updated on August 19, 2026.
